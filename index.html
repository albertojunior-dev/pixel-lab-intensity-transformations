THE METHOD

What is happening to the pixels?

The same equations shown in the controls are used by the JavaScript for every pixel.

01

Why normalize?

Dividing by 255 maps each channel to [0, 1]. The equations then work on a common scale, independent of 8-bit storage.

02

RGB versus grayscale

RGB transforms three independent channels. Grayscale first combines them into one luminance Y, then applies the same operations to that one value.

03

The three effects

Brightness shifts values; contrast expands or compresses them about 0.5; gamma curves the midtones. The order matters.

04

What is clipping?

After each operation, values outside [0, 1] are limited to 0 or 1. Detail can be lost in shadows and highlights.

05

Why does the histogram move?

It counts how many pixel components have each intensity. Shifting, stretching or curving values changes those frequencies; clipping creates peaks at the ends.

06

Back to 0–255

Only after processing, each result g is converted with I = round(255 × g). That integer is written to the final PNG.
